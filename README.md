## 🧬 About Me

I'm a **UI/UX Designer, Full-Stack Web Developer, and AI Solutions Engineer** based in **Lahore, Pakistan** — currently pursuing a **BS in Computer Science** at Green International University (GPA 3.73). I work at the intersection of **design, engineering, and intelligence**.

My work spans three disciplines: crafting pixel-perfect user experiences in **Figma**, building complete full-stack applications — from **React / Next.js** frontends to **Node.js / MongoDB** backends, including cinematic 3D and motion-driven web experiences — and developing **AI chatbots, automation systems, and integrations** for real products.

I run **[Kinetic Interface](https://radiffiaz.site/)** — my own design & development studio, where I take on real client work across UI/UX, web development, and AI solutions.
